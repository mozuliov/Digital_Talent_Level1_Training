#### How To Use Start-Stop-Continue Template

![Start-Stop-Continue](C%20Template%202.png)

To use the **Stop, Start, Continue** framework as an employee, follow these steps to connect your new digital skills to your daily workflow:

1. **Stop:** Identify inefficient or outdated habits you will phase out, such as overreliance on Excel, unnecessary manual tasks, or redundant manual double-checking.
2. **Start:** Look for ways to use technology in new ways, such as leveraging automation through IT collaboration or using GenAI and low-code tools to drive improvements.
3. **Continue:** Highlight the beneficial ways you already use technology, such as specific strategies that help you save time or collaborate better with stakeholders.
4. **Discuss:** Share these reflections during one-on-one sessions with your manager to demonstrate how your way of working has shifted
